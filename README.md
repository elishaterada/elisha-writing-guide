# Elisha's writing guide

A small public reference for helping Elisha write organic X and LinkedIn posts with dot or Claude. The voice and editorial rules live in [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md).

**Current version: 0.2.0 · 2026-11-02**

## Start here

1. At a natural stopping point during ordinary work, notice whether a genuinely useful moment might be worth sharing. Read the [canonical guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elishaterada/elisha-writing-guide/main/GUIDE.md) when considering or drafting a post, rather than on every task.
2. Bring a real moment or rough wording from the current conversation. Suggest X for a timely observation or exchange, or LinkedIn when fuller context can show a personal lesson, practical judgment, or creative AI problem-solving. Apply the guide with Elisha's current instructions.
3. Check facts and privacy, then show a draft for Elisha's review. The guide grants no authority to publish, schedule, or send posts.

Use [CLAUDE-SETUP.md](CLAUDE-SETUP.md) for a short instruction to append to existing Claude instructions and an optional Claude Code snippet. When warranted, briefly flag the platform that fits and offer at most one optional short draft across both platforms; otherwise stay silent about posting. Do not interrupt the work or force a quota. This behavior happens inside the current conversation, with no background monitoring or automatic publishing. The same fetch-and-version routine works for dot when its available tools permit it. Work-derived suggestions stay inside the approved work context for Elisha's explicit review; do not transfer them to dot or another service automatically.

## Scope and upkeep

The X mode supports casual, timely observations, experiments, tips, questions, changed decisions, and failures. The LinkedIn mode develops personal lessons and creative AI problem-solving with actual outcomes and honest limits, so prospective clients can understand Elisha's judgment. It is not generic promotional copy or a claim about algorithms, reach, or lead generation. Commercial projects can be part of the mix. Both modes are separate from the scheduled Somewhere Together video series.

Keep drafts, conversations, and working notes outside this public repo. Only add public references or general guidance explicitly intended for sharing. Do not include private repo names, local paths, client or employer information, finances, health information, credentials, or internal notes. Work-derived lessons need Elisha's review even after anonymization; combinations of details can identify a client.

For an update, review every changed file and staged diff for private material, update the guide's version and date, add a changelog entry, and create the matching version tag after review. Keep each version tag tied to its reviewed release. Do not publish a working conversation as evidence.
